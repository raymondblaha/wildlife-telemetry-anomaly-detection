COMBINED ANOMALY-DETECTION PROJECT NOTES
========================================

Project purpose
---------------
This repository contains preprocessing, movement-quality review, AKDE analysis,
and autoencoder experiments intended to identify unusual deer movement patterns.
CWD means chronic wasting disease; CWD+ and CWD- mean infected and uninfected,
respectively. WTD means white-tailed deer. MF means male and female combined.


Study-design notes
------------------

Combined_MF_Infection_10
  This is the combined male-and-female infected-animal experiment. Both the
  training dataset and the testing dataset have 10% CWD prevalence (10% of the
  animals are CWD+). The corresponding notebook is
  mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/combined_MF_Infection_10_2 copy.ipynb.

Combined_MF_Infection_20, _30, and _40
  These notebooks repeat the combined-sex infection experiment at the prevalence
  indicated by the filename. Confirm the exact animal counts in train_combined.csv
  and test_combined.csv before reporting results, because those source data are
  external to this repository.

60:40 experiment
  mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/60_40_Experiment.ipynb uses the prepared
  all_train_60_40.csv and all_test_60_40.csv datasets. The training pool is
  filtered to CWD-negative animals before its animal-level training/validation
  split. Record in the manuscript what “60:40” represents (class composition,
  sex composition, or another split), because the filename alone is ambiguous.

100 experiment
  mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/100_Experiment_2 copy.ipynb trains on the
  CWD-negative (unaffected) dataset and evaluates the learned baseline on the
  CWD-positive (affected) dataset. A grouped 80:20 training/validation split is
  made by animal ID, helping prevent observations from the same animal from
  appearing in both subsets.


Genotype-transfer experiments
-----------------------------
The WTD genotype notebooks test whether movement patterns learned from one
CWD-free PRNP genotype transfer to another CWD-free genotype. The genotype on
the LEFT side of “vs” is the training/reference genotype; the genotype on the
RIGHT side is the held-out testing/comparison genotype.

Example: Genotype_CWD_Free_Vs_Free_WTD_GG_vs_SS
  The autoencoder is trained on CWD-free GG white-tailed deer and then applied
  to CWD-free SS white-tailed deer to evaluate whether SS animals move similarly
  to the GG reference group. “Similarly” is operationalized by reconstruction
  error/anomaly classification: lower errors and fewer anomalous classifications
  indicate that the held-out genotype is represented well by movement patterns
  learned from the training genotype. This is a transfer/similarity test, not a
  test of infection effects or proof that the genotypes move identically.

Other filename comparisons follow the same convention:
  - GS_vs_GG: train/reference = CWD-free GS; test/comparison = CWD-free GG.
  - GS_vs_SS: train/reference = CWD-free GS; test/comparison = CWD-free SS.
  - SS_vs_GS: train/reference = CWD-free SS; test/comparison = CWD-free GS.

Genotype codes in the WTD notebooks are intended to map as 0 = GG, 1 = GS, and
2 = SS. Check the mapping inside each notebook before rerunning: some copied
notebooks contain stale or contradictory inline comments even where the mapping
dictionary is correct. The two “Genotype_CWD_Free_Vs_Free copy...” notebooks use
a different SS/SF/FF genotype vocabulary and should not be interpreted with the
WTD GG/GS/SS mapping.


Methods overview
----------------
1. Clean GPS records and standardize identifiers, timestamps, coordinates, CWD
   status, sex, genotype, and other metadata.
2. Regularize fix timing before deriving movement features. Recalculate movement
   metrics after regularization rather than carrying metrics forward from the
   irregular observations.
3. Engineer movement and temporal features (for example, distance/step behavior,
   turning angle, sinuosity, rolling summaries, and seasonal indicators).
4. Split data by animal ID, not by individual GPS row, so one animal cannot leak
   information between model-training and validation/test partitions.
5. Fit preprocessing objects on the training data only, then freeze and reuse the
   same encoder, scaler, feature order, and model for validation/testing.
6. Train the autoencoder to reconstruct the reference movement distribution.
   Reconstruction error is the anomaly score. Freeze the anomaly threshold from
   training/validation data before evaluating the test set.
7. Report both row-level and animal-level performance. Document the rule used to
   convert anomalous rows/windows into an animal-level classification.
8. Examine feature-level reconstruction residuals to describe which movement
   features contribute to errors. These residuals are associations and should
   not be interpreted as causal effects of CWD or genotype.

Important controls to document for reproducibility
--------------------------------------------------
- Experimental unit and prevalence: calculate prevalence from unique animals,
  not GPS rows. For the 10% experiment, verify 10% CWD+ independently in both
  training and testing after all exclusions.
- Independence: keep each animal in only one split unless the analysis is
  explicitly longitudinal and accounts for repeated measures.
- Class balance: preserve the planned animal-level prevalence after filtering.
- Sex and genotype: report their distributions by split; combined-sex analyses
  can otherwise learn sex composition rather than infection-related movement.
- Time: report sampling interval, date range, season coverage, and the length of
  movement windows. Unequal monitoring duration can change anomaly opportunity.
- Threshold: record the error statistic, percentile/decision rule, and dataset
  used to choose it. Never tune the threshold on the final test outcomes.
- Randomness: record seeds for splitting and model fitting.
- Metrics: include confusion matrix counts, sensitivity/recall, specificity,
  precision, F1, and balanced accuracy. Include uncertainty or repeated-split
  variability where possible, especially with few animals.
- Missing data: state coordinate/fix-quality exclusions and how missing movement
  features were handled. Do not allow imputation to use test-set information.
- Repeated fixes: row-level metrics are not independent animal replicates; use
  animal-level summaries for biological inference.


File descriptions
-----------------

cross_species/Mule_WTD_Normalization.Rmd
  Regularizes WTD and mule-deer GPS schedules for cross-species comparison. It
  targets 00:00/09:00/18:00 for WTD and 00:00/08:00/16:00 for mule deer, retains
  animal-days with at least two of three target fixes, and starts a new segment
  after a gap longer than 16 hours. Downstream movement metrics should be grouped
  by both id and segment_id.

cross_species/Genotype_CWD_Free_Vs_Free_WTD_*.ipynb
  CWD-free WTD genotype-transfer autoencoder experiments. Interpret the ordered
  genotype pair using the left=train, right=test convention above.

cross_species/Genotype_CWD_Free_Vs_Free copy*.ipynb
  Earlier CWD-free genotype comparison notebooks using SS/SF/FF labels. Treat
  these as a separate genotype coding scheme from the WTD GG/GS/SS notebooks.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/combined_MF_Infection_10_2 copy.ipynb
  Combined male/female experiment with 10% CWD prevalence in each of the training
  and testing datasets. Includes preprocessing, validation/cross-validation,
  testing, performance metrics, PCA/class exploration, and residual analysis.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/Combined_MF_Infection_20 copy.ipynb
mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/Combined_MF_Infection_30 copy.ipynb
mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/Combined_MF_Infection_40 copy.ipynb
  Combined-sex conditional-autoencoder experiments at the prevalence/split level
  named in each file. They also attach CWD and disease-stage labels and contain
  post-analysis of reconstruction residuals and classification errors.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/60_40_Experiment.ipynb
  Autoencoder experiment using the prepared 60:40 train/test files, with a
  grouped animal-level validation split from the CWD-negative training pool.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/100_Experiment_2 copy.ipynb
  Baseline experiment trained on CWD-free animals and tested on CWD+ animals.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/White_Tailed_Deer_REDO3.Rmd
  Preprocessing workflow copied into this experiment directory; reads telemetry,
  standardizes fields and timestamps, and removes records without coordinates.
  The filename says WTD, so verify species and input paths before reuse.

mule_deer/60:40_Experiment/Visual_Inspection_Mule_Deer.Rmd
  Manual-label review and static/interactive trajectory visualization for mule
  deer. It currently contains machine-specific input paths.

white_tailed_deer/White_Tailed_Deer_REDO3.Rmd
  WTD telemetry preprocessing, including import, field checks, timestamp parsing,
  coordinate cleaning, and preparation for downstream movement analysis.

white_tailed_deer/White_Tailed_Deer_Manual_Review.Rmd
  Manual review and visualization of individual WTD trajectories/labels. It was
  created as a lower-compute alternative and contains machine-specific paths.

white_tailed_deer/Relocation_Analysis.Rmd
  GPS fix-success diagnostic to be run before trajectory regularization. It
  summarizes fixes per deer-day, individual and seasonal/monthly differences,
  and retention under minimum-fix criteria (including >=3 versus >=4 fixes/day).

white_tailed_deer/Combo_AKDE_Results.Rmd
  Joins migratory and resident seven-day AKDE estimates and confidence limits
  back to their cleaned input records and writes enriched datasets.

white_tailed_deer/AKDE_REVIEW.Rmd
  Deep diagnostic of selected CWD+ and CWD- animals using original ctmm utilization
  distributions. Reviews AKDE estimates/intervals, effective sample sizes,
  bandwidth behavior, and whether particular migration phases drive results.

white_tailed_deer/autoencoder_100.ipynb
  Main WTD autoencoder analysis using CWD-negative training data and CWD-positive
  testing data, with movement/AKDE follow-up analyses included in later cells.

white_tailed_deer/autoencoder_100_4pings.ipynb
  WTD sensitivity analysis using six-hour resampling (four fixes per day), with
  frozen row- and animal-level flags and feature-contribution outputs.

white_tailed_deer/WTD_Autoencoder_4hours.ipynb
  WTD sensitivity analysis using a four-hour sampling/regularization workflow and
  trimmed AKDE inputs; confirm the achieved daily fix count from output summaries.


Repository-use cautions
-----------------------
- Many notebooks reference external F:, E:, LaCie, or SanDisk paths. Update paths
  or parameterize a project data root before running on another machine.
- Filenames containing “copy” or “copy 2” do not identify a definitive version.
  Compare modification dates/results and designate one canonical notebook before
  publication or archival.
- Several notebooks retain exploratory interpretations and output in saved cells.
  Rerun from a clean kernel and confirm that displayed results match current code.
- Do not infer study design solely from folder or filename labels. Preserve a
  split manifest listing animal ID, CWD status, sex, genotype, date range, and
  assigned train/validation/test partition for every reported experiment.
